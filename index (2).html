About

The short version of what I work on and how.

I'm a third-year B.Tech student in Computer Science Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at Career Point University, Kota. My work sits at the intersection of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Artificial Neural Networks, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — with a particular interest in systems that have to justify their own outputs, not just produce them.

That's shown up most directly in two projects: an evidence-first RAG editor that separates supported claims from contradicted or unverified ones, and a cloned-voice detection platform built on audio feature analysis. Alongside the ML work, I care about system design and getting the surrounding architecture — APIs, pipelines, deployment — right, not just the model.

Outside coursework, I lead the IPR Cell at my university and coordinate table tennis events, which has meant a fair amount of hackathons, competitions, and cross-functional organizing.
